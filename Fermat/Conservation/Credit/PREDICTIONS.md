# Credit-ladder predictions

Recorded before creating or editing any Lean implementation for the credit
ladder.

## Expected rung composition

- C1 should be route-neutral and structural.  A credit matrix generated from
  an orbit has empty support when no generator exists; emptiness must follow
  from absence of generation, rather than from a separately asserted
  pointwise-zero table.
- C2 should expose one generator, its recursively generated orbit, the
  sub-ledger generated by that orbit, and capacity as the index of that
  sub-ledger.  Finite certificates may prove facts about this generated
  object, but no residue matrix or list should define the orbit.
- C3 should separate the accounting drain from its arithmetic funding.
  Given the named Vandiver deep-congruence conclusion, every generated debt
  should return through the same generator and merge into the repaid ledger.
  A theorem that merely accepts an unexplained repayment function is not
  expected to count as the rung or to close an endpoint.
- The semilattice law should live on node pairs.  Debit and receivable views
  should be related by transposition/conjugation of one ledger, not by two
  independently supplied collections.

## Expected N59 instantiation

- The seed `7`, the recursion `q ↦ 2 * q + 1`, and the resulting steps
  `7 → 29 → 59` should generate the conductor tower.  The identities
  `28 = 4 * 7` and `59 = 2 * 29 + 1` should account for the rank-28 orbit
  and the two-sided ledger without 28 hand-written entries.
- The existing seven stock spines should still provide balance, gauge,
  transfer, fold, and well-founded-floor structure.  The credit rungs should
  meet them at the already-localized seam: an arithmetic repayment produces
  the strict successor consumed by the shared floor.
- The certificate prime `827` should be admissible only after proving its
  generation equation `827 = 2 * 59 * 7 + 1`.  Any finite calculation at
  827 should check a generated orbit or index statement and should not be
  promoted into the definition of credit capacity.

## Expected C4 bite

The bounded C4 bridge is expected to be the first genuinely classical seam:
an index statement about the generated cyclotomic-unit sub-ledger must imply
the plus-class-number/capacity fact used by repayment.  The structural
definition of capacity as an index should compile before this bridge.  For
N59, however, a numerical determinant or inverse cannot discharge the bridge
unless the proof also constructs:

1. the realization of the generator orbit as cyclotomic units;
2. the map from the checked finite object to the relevant sub-ledger index;
3. the Sinnott implication from that index to the required class-number
   statement.

I expect C4 in full generality to exceed this session.  If the endpoint needs
only one direction at conductor 59, the implementation should pull forward
exactly that implication and record its domain assumptions.  If even that
direction requires forbidden generic irregular machinery, the honest result
will be an obstruction localized to the C2/C4 capacity seam.

## Expected generative resistance

- Mathlib may not expose cyclotomic units in the quotient/index form required
  here.  Defining the honest generated subobject should therefore be easier
  than proving its classical index formula.
- A recursively generated orbit is likely to interact less smoothly with
  existing `Fin 28` certificate data than an enumerated vector would.  That
  friction is intentional: the adapter must prove that certificate
  coordinates evaluate the generated orbit.
- Vandiver's Lemma II is expected to be the C3 arithmetic obstruction.  The
  deep congruence
  `(1 - ζ) ^ (2 * p) ∣ u - c ^ p`
  has to force a generated unit to be a `p`-th power.  Repackaging this as a
  field, provider, or endpoint hypothesis would conceal rather than solve the
  rung.
- The two-sided view should be cheap once the ledger is one generated matrix;
  any need for separate debit and receivable seed data would signal that the
  representation has drifted back to enumeration.

## Predicted public and audit surface

I expect the route-neutral cone to expose:

- a generator-powered orbit and its recursion law;
- one two-sided credit ledger generated from that orbit;
- C1 absence-of-generator implies empty-ledger;
- C2 generated sub-ledger and capacity/index identity;
- C3 named deep-congruence repayment and ledger-drain consequences;
- `Verification.lean` with `#print axioms` for every public theorem and
  executable guards for every forbidden namespace/name.

The N59 cone should add the tower equations, generated rank/orbit facts,
certificate-generation facts actually consumed, the bounded C4 bridge if it
can be proved, and finally
`Fermat.FiftyNine.holdsAt_fiftyNine_conservation : Fermat.HoldsAt 59`.
If that endpoint cannot be proved, its place must remain empty and the
findings must identify the first unproved rung and the exact stock/credit seam.

## CREDIT-FLOW prediction — 2026-07-30

Recorded before inspecting or editing the CREDIT-FLOW implementation.

- W1's L4 law is the most likely point of resistance.  The current C2
  generator may expose a finite orbit and its generated ledger without
  exposing the λ-adic depth predicate needed to connect a deep congruence to
  zero flow.  The repair should be a generic law of the generator-derived
  flow object, not a premise attached directly to the forcing theorem.
- The generic/instance cut is most likely to fight at W2's certified
  eigenvalues.  The generic core should name the gauge action, character
  coordinates, row-scaled Vandermonde basis change, Bernoulli coefficient,
  and the exact implication furnished by a usable eigenvalue.  The thin
  instance should contain every numerical witness and certify those fields,
  without making the generic theorem mention the selected prime.
- Once W1 and W2 expose those laws at the right level, W3 should be short:
  deepness kills flow, diagonalization kills the generated coordinates, and
  C2 injectivity recognizes a p-th power.  If W3 remains arithmetically long,
  that will be evidence that a flow or capacity interface is still missing.
- The mechanical literal gate is expected to catch existing N59 leakage in
  the generic credit cone before it catches new flow code.  Any load-bearing
  occurrence should move behind named fields of the single instance record;
  campaign citations in prose may remain.
