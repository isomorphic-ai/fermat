# N7 conservation predictions

Recorded before implementation work on 2026-07-28.

## Predicted proof shape

1. The charge will live on the ring of integers of `ℚ(ζ₇)` and be the
   absolute field norm to `ℤ`.  Multiplicativity should come from the
   algebraic norm API.  The class-number-one input will be certified
   directly if the pinned library exposes a bounded class-number
   computation; otherwise it will be consumed as one precisely cited,
   bounded-role repository or Mathlib fact.
2. The gauge API will act by a map `ℤ × ℤ → Rˣ` generated by two
   independent fundamental units.  `charge_gauge_invariant` will quantify
   over both integer coordinates and prove invariance of the absolute norm
   under their product.  A separate rank/independence theorem will prevent
   this from degenerating into a renamed rank-one action.
3. This is where **rank-2 gauge should bite**: arbitrary normalization now
   has two exponents, so a representative-minimization argument cannot
   silently choose a single Pell direction.  The charge must descend to
   orbits of the full two-dimensional unit lattice.  The determinant or
   covolume controlling such a fundamental domain is the first appearance
   of the **regulator** vocabulary, even if the descent itself needs only
   orbit invariance.
4. The campaign septic fold will be stated exactly with
   `C = X² - XY + Y²`:
   `Ψ₇(X,Y) = (X-Y)⁶ + 7XYC²`.
   The factorization
   `a⁷ + b⁷ = (a+b) Ψ₇(a,-b)` (or the sign-equivalent orientation forced
   by the existing notes) will then become the ledger equation.
5. The drain quantum will be `λ = 1 - ζ₇`; its absolute norm charge will be
   exactly `7`.  Powers of `λ` will expose the literal drain scale `7^m`.
6. Primitive solutions will split along the historical first/second
   (`7 ∤ abc` / `7 ∣ abc`, or the equivalent normalized) cases.  Each
   branch must seed an iterable state whose measured charge is the same
   gauge-invariant cyclotomic norm and whose successor has strictly smaller
   charge.  Both closures will invoke the shared conservation floor.
7. The public endpoint will be
   `Fermat.Seven.holdsAt_seven_conservation : Fermat.HoldsAt 7`.

## Predicted compiler pressure

- The pinned cyclotomic API may represent `ℤ[ζ₇]` as a ring of integers
  rather than an explicit quotient.  Connecting an explicit `1 - ζ₇`
  and septic linear factor to the field norm will likely require coercion
  and integral-closure lemmas.
- Proving that two explicit cyclotomic units form the full free unit lattice
  is stronger than proving they are units.  I expect this certification,
  and the exact class-number-one boundary, to be the first serious
  library-discovery seam.
- Norm invariance itself should be short once a unit is constructed; the
  pressure is ensuring the public theorem retains two independent integer
  gauge coordinates and genuinely covers the full free quotient of the
  unit group.
- The exact signs in the historical fold are easy to misstate.  I expect
  `ring` to settle the polynomial identities only after the equation
  orientation has been compiled against the real downstream branch.
- The hard mathematical seam should be reconstruction of the
  Lamé/Lebesgue branch transformer without importing the repository's
  classical `Fermat.Seven.*` route.  I expect the compiler to expose a
  bounded set of power-allocation, coprimality, and strict-decrease
  obligations.
- If a genuine irreducible obstruction remains, the authorized fallback
  will expose those exact branch lemmas on concrete state types.  It will
  not replace the real proof by a provider, certificate, or hidden
  assumption equivalent to `Fermat.HoldsAt 7`.

## Predicted audit

- The N7 cone will import only the narrow statement boundary, the shared
  floor, bounded N2–N6 conservation support, and explicitly justified
  cyclotomic/unit/arithmetic modules.
- It will not import any classical repository `Fermat.Seven.*`
  implementation module, any Mathlib FLT module, any Ladder fold, or
  `Fermat.HoldsAt.mono_of_dvd`.
- An isolated executable verification leaf will require sentinel names from
  every forbidden route, including `Fermat.HoldsAt.mono_of_dvd`, to remain
  unknown in the transitive environment.
- Every public theorem will receive a guarded `#print axioms` check.  The
  accepted axiom surface is at most
  `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.
- The delivered cone will contain no `sorry`, `admit`, or added `axiom`;
  its independent Lake build, standard-trio audit, forbidden-name guards,
  whitespace check, and direct import scan must all be clean.
